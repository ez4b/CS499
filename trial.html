The Trial of the Group Project

The People vs. The Group Project

The Case

The project was due Friday, but one group member said, "I thought someone else was doing it."

The Accusation

The group had three days to finish the project.

The Evidence

The project contained:
47 unread messages
12 Google searches
0 completed slides

Digital Evidence

The group's project tracker showed: contributions = 0

The variable contributions represents the number of tasks completed by each group member.

The system displayed: Member has not logged in for 6 days.
Final Clue
The student was asked to press Enter to confirm the submission.

The Verdict

The jury found the student guilty of extreme procrastination.

The Sentence

Complete the project by Friday.

The judge cited The Unwritten Rules of Group Projects as supporting evidence.

Final Statement

Never trust a group chat.

Warning
